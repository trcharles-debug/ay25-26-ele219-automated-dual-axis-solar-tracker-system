\## Technical Project Title

Automated Dual-Axis Solar Tracker System



\## Problem Statement

The increasing demand for renewable energy reflects the need for more efficient solar power systems, yet fixed position solar trackers are unable to consistently capture maximum sunlight throughout the day due to changes in the sun's position. This results in reduced energy output and inefficient use of available solar resources. There is a need for an affordable and reliable solution that can adjust a panel orientation in both vertical and horizontal positions. This project addresses this by implementing a prototype dual axis solar tracker system that is designed to enhance solar efficiency and illustrate effective solar tracking.



\## Project Overview

The Automated Dual-Axis Solar Tracker System is designed to maximize the efficiency of solar energy collection by automatically adjusting the position of solar panels to follow the sun’s movement throughout the day. Unlike fixed solar panels, this system uses sensors and motors to continuously align the panels with the sun, ensuring optimal exposure and energy generation. The project aims to increase the overall power output of solar installations while minimizing manual adjustments. This system is necessary because it enhances renewable energy performance, reduces energy waste, and promotes sustainable power solutions for homes, schools, and small industries.



\## Project Scope

\-  Research solar tracking mechanisms and mathematical models to automatically track the sunlight accurately

\- Design and construct a dual-axis tracking system using sensors and microcontrollers

\- Construct a light sensing module using 4 Light Dependent Resistors (LDRs) separated by a cross-divider

\- Construct a stable mechanical frame that supports dual axis movement while holding the solar panel and components securely in place

\- Use an open source IOT platform to display information and adjust parameters to locate the panel accordingly

-Test and analyze system performance under different environmental conditions to evaluate tracking accuracy and system responsiveness

-Improve system stability, accuracy, and energy efficiency

-Document the entire design, testing, and implementation process
